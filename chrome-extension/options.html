Homes.ph Publisher

One click on a time publishes the article on the dash.homes.ph board (Clear all, then only the page's sites) and sends its Facebook post with the News Article picture from Homes.ph SocMedSystem. The panel shows only the pages assigned to you in the app (Settings → Assigned pages).

Homes.ph SocMedSystem

Checking…

Homes.ph SocMedSystem (Daily Carousel Maker before 1.50) must be open on this computer. Connect once: the app asks you to allow it. Then each article you publish here is also added to News Article (headline, photo, posting time), its template is saved in your download folder and attached to the Facebook post.

How it works

minutes or less before the slot's time (or past it): the Facebook post goes out right away instead of being scheduled.

Pages

Every page that can be assigned (yours are marked). Target Hub = the row's name on the board. Publish to = the only sites ticked after Clear all (Main News Portal always; a Homes.ph page: Homes; FHI: fhiglobal and Rentsouq). Facebook Page = the only Page ticked in Schedule Post. Article address = where the article is on the brand's website (the READ MORE link).

Carousels

Only when the carousel is assigned to you. Post opens the Sites board's Create Post (Send to Facebook): the slides (1.png = the cover), under Others every city picked at 11:00 PM on its day, every Homes.ph Page ticked (Main Page = "Homes.ph"), the caption from the app, then Post now. Old way (above): the checklist's Facebook pages in posts of 4 on Facebook Autopost; Post fills in Schedule Post: those Pages ticked (Main Page = the Page "Homes.ph", ID 253657525049795; a city = the Homes.ph Page with its name), the carousel's caption, its slides in order (1.png = the cover, also saved in your download folder), 11:00 PM on its day. You click Schedule Post; then the Pages are ticked on the app's checklist and turn green here.
