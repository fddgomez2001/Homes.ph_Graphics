Homes.ph Publisher (Chrome extension)

1. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions
2. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose THIS folder ("Chrome Extension", the one with manifest.json in it).
   Choose the folder itself: don't open a folder inside it.
4. Click the extension's Details > Extension options > Connect, then Allow in Homes.ph SocMedSystem.
5. Stay signed in to dash.homes.ph and homes.ph in Chrome.

After Homes.ph SocMedSystem updates, click the reload arrow on the extension's card in chrome://extensions.

Carousels: the panel also shows the day's carousel from Homes.ph SocMedSystem. Click "Post 1" (then Post 2…):
up to 4 Facebook Pages, the caption, the slides in order (1.png = cover) and 11:00 PM are filled in for you.
Check it and click Schedule Post: the Pages are ticked on the app's posting checklist.

Trending tab: the day's Daily Trend News posters from Homes.ph SocMedSystem's Poster Maker, per page. Click a time:
the poster, its AI caption, its time and its Page are filled in on Facebook Autopost. You click Schedule Post.

Ads tab: the advertisements scheduled in Homes.ph SocMedSystem (Advertisement page), the same way.

Quotes tab: "Schedule a quote" for PicklePlay, FHI, Caraga or Gensan (picture, caption, time: 4:00 PM unless you
change it), "Done setup", then "Post". The small check box next to a post marks it posted when it went out another way.

Reports tab: click "Start Your Day" (stay signed in to dtr.homes.ph). It only adds today's tasks in My Tasks that
aren't there yet, To do or Done today (In progress, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM; Saturday 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM); HR's "Search a
Trending News Daily" is set In progress. Nothing is reported by itself: once everything a task reports is posted, its
"Send report" button shows. It posts one update per post, then Done, then goes back to My Tasks, and the pill turns
green. Before everything is posted (time out, Saturday's half day) the button reads "Send 3 posted": it reports only
what's posted so far, then Done. With nothing posted it reads "Mark Done" (Done, no update).

Daily report: once every task in Reports is Done, "Send daily report" shows. It downloads Today's report from My Tasks
(the panel says where it's saved), then in the report chat on Messenger it types the greeting ("Good Evening" Monday to Friday, "Good Afternoon" on Saturday), mentioning each person
(picked from Messenger's list and checked), sends it, then sends the report picture. Stay signed in to messenger.com.
Nothing is sent when a mention can't be made: the panel says what to fix.
