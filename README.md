# Daily Carousel Maker · Homes.ph

A Windows desktop app (and web app, and Android app) for planning, designing and exporting Homes.ph Facebook/Instagram carousels every day.

- **Home.** Every card lines up: the title always takes two lines, then the date, the status, and the **Checklist** and **PNGs** buttons along the bottom. The first page you see: one month at a time (pick it with the arrows or the month list; it opens on this month), carousels in publication order: October 31, November 1, November 2… Each is a pile of its slides, like stacked photos: the cover on top with its real cards tilted underneath, sharp corners, 5 per row. Posted (Published) carousels are greyed out. Beside the **⋯** menu: a **check** button (mark as posted now, click again to undo) and a **download** button (every slide as its own PNG: 1.png is the cover, then 2.png, 3.png…). Search looks through every month.
- **Posting checklist (17 Facebook pages).** Every carousel has a checklist of the Homes.ph pages: Main Page, Ozamiz, Dumaguete, BGC Taguig, Cebu, Pampanga, Cavite, Iloilo, Bacolod, Davao, Palawan, Bohol, Makati, CDO, Gensan, Caraga and Leyte. Tick a page after posting on it: the person signed in and the time are saved (e.g. *Posted: Frank Gomez | Peter Yares*). The count shows *6/17*; while some pages are done the carousel is **Partially published**, and once all 17 are ticked it becomes **Published** (greyed out on Home). Unticking a page of a finished checklist makes it not posted again. Open it from the ✓ button on a Home card, the carousel preview, or the website.
- **Floating checklist.** **Floating checklist** (in the carousel preview, or *Open in a small window* on the website) opens the checklist in its own small window that stays on top of the browser while you post on Facebook. Its picker shows every carousel with ✓ (posted), ◐ (part-way) or ○ (not yet).
- **Team.** Each person signs in with their own account. **Settings → Team**: set *Your name* (shown as *Made by* and *Posted by*) and add teammates by the email of their account; everyone in the team sees, edits and posts the team's carousels. Only a carousel's owner can delete it for good.
- **Three brands: Homes.ph, FHI Global Property and PicklePlay.** Every carousel template comes in three versions with the same layout as the Homes.ph template: **Homes.ph** (the default: navy, gold swoosh and headline), **FHI Global Property** (deep navy #001F3F, champagne-gold swoosh, gold gradient headline, cream card subtitle, FHI emblem, fhiglobal.ae footer) and **PicklePlay** (royal blue #0A2CAA, lime swoosh, lime gradient headline, light-lime card subtitle, PicklePlay logo, pickleplay.ph footer).
  - **Home** has a tab for each brand (**Homes.ph** first, with the number of carousels); each tab shows only that brand's carousels, and **Create New Carousel** from a tab starts with that brand.
  - **Create New Carousel → Brand** picks the template (Homes.ph by default). The suggested day, the *one carousel a day* check, Priority and the *featured before* checks count only that brand's carousels; the AI (topics, web search, article) writes for that brand (Dubai and the UAE for FHI, pickleball for PicklePlay).
  - **Editor → Templates → Carousel brand** switches a whole carousel to another brand: the cover and every content card get the brand's template (logo, swoosh, colors, gradient, footer); the words and photos are kept, anything you added on top stays, and other slides (text, full-image, quote cards) don't change. One **Ctrl+Z** undoes it. **Add card** adds a card in the carousel's brand, and the editor's AI (caption, cover subtitle, card descriptions) writes for that brand.
  - The **Daily Report**'s carousel task (*Homes.ph Social Media Pages*) counts the Homes.ph carousels only. The posting checklist (17 Homes.ph pages) is the same for every carousel.
- **Carousel from a topic (AI web search).** In **Create New Carousel** → **Use AI** → **Search a topic**: type a topic like *Top Restaurants In Cebu City 2026 For Filipino Cuisine* or *Top 2 Resorts Each In Luzon, Visayas And Mindanao* (or click **Suggest topics** for timely ideas for this month that you haven't done yet), choose the number of cards and **Search the web**. Groq searches the web and lists the places it found, each with its facts and its source (a link to open and check). Groups are respected (2 per region…). **Make the carousel** then writes the cover, caption and cards from the ticked places only.
  - **No repeats:** a topic close to an earlier carousel shows a warning; the AI is told the places your recent carousels already featured and must skip them; any place featured before is flagged (in the list and on the cards) and starts unticked.
  - **Checks:** a place whose source the search didn't really open shows *source not confirmed*, and a location that looks wrong (e.g. *Boracay is in Visayas, not Luzon*) shows *check the location*; both start unticked. Tick any place you still want.
  - **Groq's free plan** allows about one web search every 2–3 minutes (a search uses about 20,000 of the 8,000 tokens a minute it allows). When one Groq model is busy the app switches to the other, and it waits out short pauses by itself; otherwise it says how long to wait.
- **Carousel from an article (AI).** In **Create New Carousel**, **Make it from an article**: paste the article (or its link, or use **From a link**), choose how many cards (2–10), and **Write the carousel**. The AI reads it and fills in **Headline 1**, **Headline 2**, the **Subtitle** and the **Caption**, and writes every card's **title**, **subtitle** and a **2–3 line description**, only from the article: real names, places, numbers and dates, never made up (if the article has fewer items, you get fewer cards). Descriptions longer than 3 lines on the card are written again, shorter. Check or change the cards, then **Create carousel**: the cover and all the cards are made; add the photos in the editor. Uses your own AI key (Settings → Poster Maker AI) or the built-in Groq.
- **New carousels, one a day at 11:00 PM.** **Create New Carousel** starts on the next day that has no carousel yet (e.g. Oct 30 – Nov 3 done → Nov 4) at 11:00 PM; pick another day if you like. Picking a day that already has a carousel offers **Priority** (on by default): the new carousel takes that day and the carousels already scheduled move one day later, the next ones too, up to the first free day (posted ones never move; the dialog lists what moves before you create it). Each carousel shows **Made early · N days ahead**, **Made on the day** or **Made late**, and **by** whoever made it.
- **Carousel preview.** Clicking a carousel on Home shows it like the posting sheet: the cover large on the left, the cards three across beside it, the caption underneath, and the pages checklist on the side. Click a slide to edit it.
- **Dark mode (default).** The app, the floating editor and the posting website open in an elegant navy dark mode. The **sun / moon** button (top bar of every page, the editor bar, the website header and the sign-in page) switches to light mode in one click; the choice is remembered on that computer and applies to every open window, and the desktop app's title bar and splash screen follow it. Exported images are never affected.
- **Android app (APK).** *Homes.ph Carousels* for phones and tablets: the carousels ready to post (swipe the slides, **Save all slides to Photos** numbered 1 = cover, **Share…** straight to Facebook or Messenger, one-tap caption copy, the 17-page checklist), the day's **News** posts for PicklePlay, FHI and Homes.ph CARAGA (save or share each), the **Daily Report** image, and **Settings** (dark/light, your name, team). Same account and data as the desktop app. See *Android app* below.
- **Posting website.** A separate light website (no editor) for whoever posts: the carousels ready to post, grouped by day, each with its slides, caption (one-click copy) and downloads. **Mark as posted** saves the date and time; the Carousel Maker then shows the carousel as Published with that time. See *Posting website* below to deploy it.
- **Daily Report (Daily Tasks image).** Sidebar → **Daily Report** (or **Generate report** on News Article): pick a day and download one PNG laid out like the Figma "Daily Tasks" board. **DONE** shows the day's *Pickleplay.ph Article Posting*, *FHI Global Property Article Posting* (each post under its 9 AM / 11 AM / 3 PM / 7 PM / 10 PM label; wide FHI posts at their full 1742 px width) and *Homes.ph CARAGA Article Posting* (11 AM / 1:30 PM / 6 PM / 8 PM / 10 PM), then *Scheduled Carousel Posting (Homes.ph Pages)* — the pages it went out on (with the Homes.ph logo), all its slides and the caption. **PENDING** lists what's still to do: empty article slots (today and tomorrow; *Pickleplay.ph & FHI Global Property* share a line when the same times are missing, *Homes.ph CARAGA* has its own), carousels not (fully) posted with the pages left, and a carousel not made yet. Standard size is 5082 px wide; Full size is the Figma's 10164 px. The page itself is a dashboard: the day and the download on top, a card per brand and for the carousel with progress bars, each brand's posts in a neat row by time (empty times as dashed slots), the carousel's slides, the pages it went out on and its caption, then Pending grouped by day — with the Daily Tasks image beside it (click any picture, or **View full size**, to see it large).
- **Floating report.** Daily Report → **Floating report** opens a small window that stays on top (in the desktop app; a pop-up window in a browser) with the day's report lines, each one **a click away on the clipboard** (with Unicode bold that stays bold when pasted), in three tabs:
  - **News Article Posting**, for Pickleplay.ph, FHI Global and Homes.ph CARAGA: the **logo** copies *Daily Social Media Posting – Pickleplay.ph*; the **Details** box copies *Posted and scheduled daily articles on the Pickleplay.ph social media page at 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 3:00 PM, 7:00 PM, and 10:00 PM.* (plain text) (CARAGA: 11:00 AM, 1:30 PM, 6:00 PM, 8:00 PM, and 10:00 PM); each **time** (green when it has an article) copies `[𝟵 𝗔𝗠] - <headline>`, a blank line and `𝗔𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗹𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝗻𝗸: https://www.pickleplay.ph/news/<the-headline-as-a-slug>` (FHI: `https://fhiglobal.ae/news/…`, CARAGA: `https://homes.ph/news/…`), **and saves the post's picture** in `D:\DAILY REPORT\New Article Posting\Pickleplay` (or `FHI Global`, `Homes.ph CARAGA`). A headline typed a moment ago in the app is picked up when you click its time.
  - **Daily Trend News**, the Poster Maker's posters of the day for Pickleplay.ph, FHI Global and Homes.ph CARAGA, numbered by time: **[ Pickleplay.ph DTN 1 ]** copies *𝗣𝗶𝗰𝗸𝗹𝗲𝗽𝗹𝗮𝘆.𝗽𝗵 - 𝗗𝗮𝗶𝗹𝘆 𝗧𝗿𝗲𝗻𝗱 𝗡𝗲𝘄𝘀 𝟭 [11/6/2026]*, the headline in bold with the subtext after it in brackets, and *𝗗𝗲𝘁𝗮𝗶𝗹𝘀:* 1–2 sentences on what the news is about and why it's a trend, **and saves the poster** in `D:\DAILY REPORT\Daily Trend News\<brand>`. The details are written by AI with the poster (**Write from article**), or here (**Write with AI**), and can be typed; they're saved with the poster. A poster without details gets them written by AI when you copy it. Homes.ph posters show under their page (1.38): *Homes.ph CARAGA*, *Homes.ph Gensan*, and a group for each other page with posters that day (*Homes.ph Cebu DTN 1*, saved in `…\Daily Trend News\Homes.ph Cebu`).
  - **Carousel**: the **Homes.ph logo** copies *Daily Carousel Content Creation and Scheduling – Homes.ph Social Media Pages*; **Details** copies *Created and scheduled daily carousel content for all Homes.ph social media pages, with posts set to publish every day at 11:00 PM.* Each carousel of the day shows its schedule line: *𝗖𝗮𝗿𝗼𝘂𝘀𝗲𝗹 𝗦𝘂𝗰𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗳𝘂𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗦𝗰𝗵𝗲𝗱𝘂𝗹𝗲𝗱:*, the day and time (*7/11/2026 | 11 PM*), the title (Headline 1 and 2) in bold and every page with ✅ (⏳ for a page not posted yet). Its **Copy** unlocks once all 17 pages are ticked on the posting checklist; it copies the line and saves the whole carousel (cover and cards) **as one picture** in `D:\DAILY REPORT\Carousel`.
  - **Saved pictures** are always under 4.5 MB: PNG when it fits, otherwise a high-quality JPEG at full size (only made smaller when even that is too big). Copying the same post again replaces its picture (one file per post). The folder can be changed in Settings → Downloads & saving → **Daily report folder**; a computer without a D: drive uses `Downloads\DAILY REPORT`. In a browser the pictures are normal downloads.
  The bottom strip shows what was copied last and where its picture was saved (**Show** opens the folder). The window refreshes when something is saved in the app, and remembers its place on the screen.

- **Trash.** Deleting a carousel asks you to type its title, then moves it to the **Trash**, where it stays for 30 days. From there you can **Restore** it or **Delete forever** (again after typing the title). A deleted carousel disappears from every list at once: Home, Calendar, Unscheduled, Day Board and All Content.

- **Title Auto-Fit (your Figma plugin, built in).**
  - Each title line stays on **one line** inside 1062 px. It shrinks from its max size (172 / 148 / 50 px) until it fits, and never grows past it.
  - The gap between the two headline lines is 5 px at full size and shrinks with the text, following the plugin formula: `round(5 × ratio²) + extra gap`. Here `ratio` is how far the more-shrunk headline is below its own max size.
  - The subtitle stays 20 px below, and the Title Block stays centered at y 598.
  - It runs live as you type. The plugin's options are in the cover's slide settings: *Same size for white & gold lines*, *Keep Title Block centered* and *Extra line gap*. Set your defaults in **Settings → Brand**.

### Install

1. Build the installer (see *Building the installer* below) or use the one already built: `dist\Daily-Carousel-Maker-Setup-1.35.0.exe`. Installing a newer version replaces the old one and keeps your settings.

### First run (the welcome setup, 1.35)

A new install opens a short welcome setup. No account is needed:

1. **Welcome**: the app is for internal use only (built by Frank Gomez for the team), and what it can do. *Skip* or *Continue*.
2. **This app uses AI**: the installer has no AI key, so each person connects their own. Paste the key and click **Connect**: the app checks it, runs a short test and saves it. Groq, Gemini and Perplexity can also search the web with the same key; with the others a free Tavily key is needed for trending news. Continue stays off until an AI is connected.
3. **Where should your work be saved?**
   - **On this computer** (recommended): everything, photos too, goes into a folder, by default `Documents\Daily Carousel Maker` (*Change…* picks another). Carousels are in its `projects` folder, photos in `images`. To back up, copy the folder.

Later: **Settings → Where your work is saved** (open or change the folder, *Run the welcome setup again*) and **Settings → AI & web search**:

### Building the installer

```bash
npm install
npm run desktop:dist      # → dist/Daily-Carousel-Maker-Setup-<version>.exe
```

`npm run desktop` builds and opens the desktop app without packaging it. `npm run desktop:start` reopens the last build.

After building, `scripts/dist-desktop.mjs` writes the installer's SHA-256 checksum next to it (`…Setup-<version>.exe.sha256.txt`; check a download with PowerShell `Get-FileHash <file>`), scans it with Microsoft Defender (the build stops if Defender flags it) and prints whether it's signed.

**Downloading on other computers (SmartScreen, antivirus).** An installer without a code-signing certificate makes Windows show *"Windows protected your PC"* the first time (**More info → Run anyway**); that's Windows not knowing the publisher yet, not a virus finding. On Windows 11 with *Smart App Control* turned on, unsigned apps are blocked outright. The fix is to **sign** the installer:

1. Get a code-signing certificate in the company's name (OV, from a certificate authority such as Sectigo, DigiCert, SSL.com or Certum; since 2023 they come on a USB token or as a cloud signing service). Microsoft's Azure Artifact Signing doesn't take individuals or organizations in the Philippines yet.
2. Build with it: a certificate file → set `CSC_LINK` (path or base64 of the .pfx) and `CSC_KEY_PASSWORD`; a token or cloud certificate installed in Windows → set `WIN_CERT_SUBJECT` (its name) or `WIN_CERT_SHA1` (its thumbprint). Then `npm run desktop:dist`: the app and installer are signed (SHA-256) and time-stamped.
3. SmartScreen builds trust for a signed publisher over the first downloads. If an antivirus or Defender ever flags the app, report it as a false positive (Microsoft: microsoft.com/wdsi/filesubmission).

Since 1.35 the installer carries **no API keys** (no Groq, Tavily or Cloudinary key or secret): only non-secret defaults (`CLOUDINARY_FOLDER`, `CLOUDINARY_UPLOAD_PRESET`, `GROQ_MODEL`). Each person sets up their own AI and storage in the welcome setup. Installs that already had keys keep them.

## How it works

- **Slides** are stored as editable JSON: elements (image, text, shape) in layer order, with positions, fonts, gradients, crop and logo settings. Nothing is saved only as a flattened image.
- **Title Auto-Fit.** Each title line is measured with the real font on a single line, at its max size. If it's too wide, the size becomes `floor(max × 1067 / width)` and then steps down 1 px until it fits (minimum 8 px). This is exactly the plugin's `fitSize()`. Headline line height is 100%; the subtitle keeps its 63 / 50 line height.
- **Glows** (radial gradient + `blur(156.5px)`) are painted with the browser's own canvas blur, so they match Figma's CSS. The same bitmap is used in the editor and the export.
- **Text auto-arrange.** The cover Title Block stays centered where Figma places it. The card text stays bottom-aligned, so longer text pushes the block up instead of overlapping. Empty lines are skipped, which is how the no-subtitle layout works. Dragging one of those text boxes by hand turns auto-arrange off for that slide; switch it back on in the slide settings.
- **Logo color.** Before rendering, the app draws only the layers *behind* the logo in its 88 × 86 area (photo, gradients, top band), measures their average luminance, and picks the logo with the better contrast: blue if luminance is above 0.27, otherwise white. With the navy top band this is almost always the white H; very bright photos still get the blue logo. The blue logo fills 65/72 × 63/70 of the slot, centered, like in Figma.
- **Export** renders each slide offscreen at exactly 1254 × 1254, after every font and full-resolution image has loaded. No editor UI can appear in the file. Text that would be cut off, or that runs into the logo or footer, triggers a warning first.
- **Caching.** Data is cached in memory and in IndexedDB, keyed by your user id and the app version. Screens show the cached copy first and refresh it in the background, on window focus, and after any change. Day Board slides and their rendered previews are keyed by each carousel's last change, so they are reused until you edit it. The sign-in check on each page only looks for the session cookie (no network call); the database still checks every request (Row Level Security). The desktop app starts the window while its server boots and keeps Node's compile cache between launches.
- **Backups** are ZIP files: `backup.json` (format `homes-carousel-backup`, version 1) with every carousel's details and full slide designs, plus `images/` with the original files. Restoring uploads the images again (identical files are reused), gives the slides new ids, and points them at the stored originals.
- **Floating editor.** The editor publishes its slides to the desktop app, which relays them to the floating window. The floating window sends back only small edits: text, caption, slide choice, and pasted images. Each message is accepted only from the window it belongs to.
- **Autosave** runs about 1 second after you stop editing, one request at a time. Each save includes its base revision, so an older save can never overwrite newer edits. When offline, it retries automatically and keeps a local backup that it offers to restore.

A change to the extension reaches the phone with the next APK. Tests: `tests/mobile-publisher.test.ts`.

### Building the APK

Needs JDK 21 and the Android SDK (platform 36, build-tools 36): set `JAVA_HOME` and `ANDROID_HOME`, or put them in `E:\AndroidBuild\jdk` and `E:\AndroidBuild\sdk`.

```bash
npm run mobile:apk     # static export → secret check → Capacitor sync → signed release APK in dist\
npm run mobile:build   # only update the Android project (android/), e.g. to open it in Android Studio
```

- The app is a static export of one page (`src/app/page.mobile.tsx`, built with `MOBILE_BUILD=1`, see `next.config.ts`) packed by Capacitor; it contains only `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and the publishable key from `.env.local`. The build stops if any other `.env` value appears in it.
- **Signing key:** `android/release-key/` (`homesph-release.jks` and `keystore.properties` with its password) is created once and is **never committed**. Keep a backup of that folder somewhere safe: Android only installs updates signed with the same key. If it's lost, uninstall the app and install the new one.
- Icons and launch screens: `python scripts/android-assets.py` (from `public/brand/app-icon-512.png`).

## Security notes

- Only the Supabase **publishable** key is in the browser bundle. The Cloudinary key and secret stay on the server: in the desktop app they live in your local settings file, and the Settings page only reports whether they are set.
- All database access is protected by Row Level Security. API routes re-check the signed-in user.
- Image links are downloaded by the server only for signed-in users, with a rate limit, http/https only, standard ports, at most 3 redirects, a 15-second timeout and the 25 MB limit. The bytes must really be a JPG, PNG or WebP. Addresses on your computer or private network (localhost, 10.x, 192.168.x, 169.254.x, …) are refused, also after DNS lookups and redirects.
- The device cache only holds your own rows, scoped by user id, and is wiped on sign-out.
- Teams are enforced in the database: a carousel, its slides and its checklist are visible only to its owner and the owner's teammates. Teammates are added only through *Add teammate* (by the email of an existing account), a tick always records the person signed in and the current time, and nobody can change a carousel's owner or maker.
- The Android app holds only the Supabase URL and publishable key (checked at build time); everything else is Row Level Security, exactly like the website. It saves images only into its own *Pictures/Homes.ph* album and needs no storage permission. Its signing key stays on the build computer (gitignored).
- The posting website uses only the Supabase publishable key and Row Level Security: it reads your own carousels and changes only the posted status and time. It needs no server secrets.
- The Groq API key is server-only (no `NEXT_PUBLIC_`). The AI route accepts only its fixed writing tasks (no free-form prompts), for signed-in users, with size limits and a per-user rate limit.
- A Tavily key saved in Settings → Poster Maker AI → Web search is kept the same way (this computer only) and travels only with web searches to the app's server, which passes it to Tavily for that search. Since 1.35 the desktop installer no longer carries the app's own Tavily or Groq key.
- A Poster Maker AI key (Settings → Poster Maker AI) is kept in this computer's app storage, never in the database. It travels with each Poster Maker AI request to the app's own server, which uses it for that one call to the chosen provider's fixed address and doesn't store or log it.
- Without an account (desktop), the work is plain files in the chosen folder. The local server answers those requests only from the app's own pages: a request from another site or another host name (a website open in a browser, DNS rebinding) is refused, and only the app's photo files (`<hash>.png|jpg|webp`) are served from the folder.
- Web search keys travel only with web searches, each checked for its service's format on the server (`x-dcm-search-engine`, `x-dcm-perplexity-key`, `x-dcm-groq-key`, `x-dcm-gemini-key`, `x-dcm-tavily-key`, `x-dcm-serpapi-key`, `x-dcm-meta-token`), and are used for that one request. The 30-minute search cache keeps only a short fingerprint of a key, never the key. The Meta token reads Facebook Pages only through Meta's official Graph API, with the permissions Meta gave it; nothing on Facebook is scraped. Gemini's Google Search links (vertexaisearch redirects) are resolved with a HEAD request without following them.
- The person's own AI and Tavily keys are kept in the app's page storage and, in the desktop app, also as a copy encrypted by Windows (`safeStorage`) in `setup.json`, so they survive a change of the app's port. They are never written to the work folder.
- The desktop window is sandboxed, with no Node.js access for the page. It never navigates away from the app, and external links open in your browser.
- If a credential was ever committed or shared, **rotate it**: generate a new Cloudinary API secret, and new Supabase API keys.
